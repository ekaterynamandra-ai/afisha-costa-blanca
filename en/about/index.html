Kate — two lives, one place

I live in Spain, run a Telegram community, and build an AI Studio with my husband. I keep both threads here because I live both.

Why this site exists

I moved, figured out the paperwork, found a rhythm of working by the sea — and in parallel my husband and I build an AI studio for businesses and teach AI to people. This site is where those two sides stop looking like two separate topics.

I publish what helped me and the people moving alongside me: how to move (visas, NIE, banking, healthcare), how to work here (autónomo, taxes, coworkings, seasonal work), how to grow with AI (tools, agents, real cases), how to live (places, events, nature).

AI Studio & learning

With my husband we run YESWorkflow — a studio that ships custom AI automations for businesses: voice bots, team assistants, integrations. And in parallel aipack.live — a learning community with courses on ChatGPT, n8n, Claude Code, Codex and working with AI agents.
